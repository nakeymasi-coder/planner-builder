# 🦋 Glam Planner Builder

Glam's fully customizable planner builder — pick a planner type and it builds the cover, back cover, dividers and the complete insert set for that type.

**Planner types:** Agenda, Finance, Business, Social Media, Content Creator, Travel, Meal, Mom Binder, and Self-Care — plus notepad pages (lined, dotted, blank, checklist) you can add anywhere.

**Themes & styles:** Glam Sapphire & Pearl, LV-inspired and GG-inspired luxury looks, Noir, Blush, Halloween, Christmas, and original character-inspired themes (cartoon pup, lazy orange cat, yellow canary, blue forest friends, whirlwind rascal), a create-your-own character studio, multiple art styles, a 30-sticker tray, and 14 typography styles named from the Glam Prompt Vault. Picking a theme restyles the cover, lettering, colors and pages together.

**Usable, not decoration:** every page is click-and-type — editable fields, tables and checklists — with fully hyperlinked side tabs and a clickable index, a real 2026 calendar with 2026 U.S. holidays, and a Password Keeper section (remove that page from copies you sell).

**Install it on your phone:** open the live page, tap the ⋮ menu (Android) or Share (iPhone), and choose **Add to Home Screen**. It installs as its own app — "Glam Planner" — with its own sapphire butterfly icon.
