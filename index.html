Planner Architect — Master Plan

Cleaned up, the way you asked it — this is the blueprint this builder follows:

1 — Pick a planner, get the whole set

Cover + back cover + a full insert set for the type you choose: Finance, Business, Social Media, Content Creator, Travel, Meal, Mom Binder, Self-Care, or Agenda. Core 2026 monthly / weekly / daily pages and a Password Keeper ride along with every set.

2 — Theme changes everything at once

Pick a theme and the cover material, colors, page accents and title lettering all match it — luxury house-inspired looks, seasonal Halloween & Christmas, and original character themes made for selling. Names of licensed cartoon characters are personal-use only; the sellable themes here are original, inspired-by characters.

3 — Everything is click-and-type

Every title, table, checklist and note line on every page is editable. Add lined, dotted, blank or checklist notepad pages anywhere. Tap stickers into any page. Nothing is just decoration.

4 — Fully hyperlinked

The index below and the tabs above the pages jump straight to every section — the way a hyperlinked digital planner works in GoodNotes / Notability.
